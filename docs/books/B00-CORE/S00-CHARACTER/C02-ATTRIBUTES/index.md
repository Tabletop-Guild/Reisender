# The Attribute System

## Introduction
To keep it simple, Attributes are the basic building blocks of a character. These are broken down into Primary Attributes, Secondary Attributes, and Tertiary Attributes.

### Primary Attributes
These are your big ones, strength, agility, etc.

Just about every game, with some exceptions, has some in some form or another within reason. These values are generated with dice and they have some impact, directly on indirectly, on pretty much everything else in the game. As you can imagine these are extremely important.

### Secondary Attributes
These are your medium ones, your health points, mana points, speed, etc. 

These are just as important as the primary attributes. The main difference is that instead of being rolled these values are derived based upon a combination of the Primary Attributes and other factors that may affect your character. 

### Tertiary Attributes
These are your small ones, hair colour, weight, sex, gender, etc.

While your tertiary attributes seldom impact gameplay on a strictly mechanical level, they are important in their own way. These attributes can often have interesting consequences. Being taller might allow one to reach higher or farther, whereas being small might allow you to fit in tighter spaces. Some people might have an affinity or aversion for different sexes and genders. Tertiary attributes are more likely to impact roleplay and how a Game Master makes use of these attributes can have surprising impact.

## The Primary Attributes

### Strength (STR)
Strength is a measure of a character's physical strength.

### Dexterity (DEX)
Dexterity is a measure of a character's fine motor control and coordination.

### Agility (AGI)
Agility is a measure of a character's flexibility and mobility.

### Conditioning (CON)
Conditioning is a measure of a character's overall physical fitness and health.

### Reasoning (REA)
Reasoning is a measure of a character's ability to learn, understand, and evaluate things.

### Spirit (SPR)
Spirit is a measure of a character's spiritual strength. 

### Determination (DET)
Determination is a measure of a character's mental strength. 

### Perception (PER)
Perception is a measure of a character's ability to perceive their surroundings.

### Charisma (CHA)
Charisma is a measure of a character's force of personality.
