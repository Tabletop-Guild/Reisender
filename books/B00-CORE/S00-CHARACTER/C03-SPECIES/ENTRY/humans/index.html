    The Human

    Overview

    The Human is probably the most widespread of the world's many inhabitants. They make their homes wherever they feel and tend to pop up in the oddest of places for the strangest of reasons. It's not uncommon to see them at home deep in the cities of elves, the forests of dryads, or within the narcisse encampments. Like other species, their instinctual need for companionship leads them to seek out others of similar mindset and groups where they can easily integrate themselves; Unlike other species, they often have few if any qualms with who they make their companions, let alone their bed mates, which allows them to easily stray from their primary population centres.

    Compared to others, the humans live relatively short lives and it shows. While individuals vary greatly in their temperament, the average human is volatile, impatient, and passionate to a fault. The truth of it is that humans are acutely aware of their limited time and tend to try to make the best of it and live without regrets. Desperate for purpose they latch onto people, places, ideals, even concepts. When something a human has come to hold dear is threatened, they react, in what is probably the biggest understatement of the century, extremely poorly. A human wronged has an unparalleled capacity for violence unadulterated by thoughts of restraint or reason. A human wronged will make you pay even if it's the legitimate last thing they do.

    Don't Fear the Reaper

    Whenever you are called upon to roll a resistance check, and the penalty for failure is death or unconsciousness, you may re-roll the check as if you had used one destiny point for a re-roll. You may do this at no cost regardless of the number of destiny points you have remaining.
